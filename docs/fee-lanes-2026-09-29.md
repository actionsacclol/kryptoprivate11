# Fee lanes — the lean lane for scripts (2026-09-29)

## Why

Swarm 4's cost-floor agent itemised all 95 real round trips the callout farm
made (fills.json, reconciled to the lamport). They deployed 1.367 SOL and lost
0.693 SOL:

| component | SOL | share of the loss |
|---|---|---|
| priority fee | 0.344 | 50 % |
| price move | 0.251 | 36 % |
| Jito / Helius tips | 0.056 | 8 % |
| pump fee (1.25 %/side) | 0.031 | 4 % |
| Krypt, rent, base, impact | 0.011 | 2 % |

The priority fee is the fixed floor in `priorityFeeSolFor` (engine.ts): every
Solana buy pays at least 0.001 SOL and every sell at least 0.002, then a sell
bumps urgency one step and tips go to Jito at p95. Those floors were chosen for
landing speed, for sniping and for stops. What the rest of the market pays is
different: 64 sampled pump curve trades on 09-16 paid a median 32,110 lamports
of priority (0.000032 SOL), 84 % paid less than our buy floor, 70 % sent no
tip, and they landed. The app's own transactions consume a median 85.7k CU on
a buy and 55.1k on a sell-and-close under a 120k limit.

On a 0.03 SOL bag the fast lane's zero-move round trip costs about 14 %; with
the fees near their market level it costs about 2.6 %, which is pump's own
1.25 %/side and little else.

## What changed

- **`shared/exitBudget.ts`**: `FeeLane = 'fast' | 'lean'`.
  `leanPriorityFeeSol(p50, cu)` prices the compute unit at the live estimate's
  median, clamped to 50,000–500,000 micro-lamports (no floor, no urgency bump),
  which is about 0.000006–0.00006 SOL on a 120k limit. `leanExec` turns off
  Jito and the Helius sender and sets `mevMode: 'off'`. The fee-share guard is
  per lane: the smallest unattended live buy is 0.03 SOL on the fast lane and
  0.0025 SOL on the lean one, judged at the lean ceiling price on PumpSwap's
  250k limit so it is never optimistic.
- **`electron/engine/engine.ts`**: `testTrade` and `manualSell` take
  `feeLane`. A lean buy goes out at the lean fee with no tips. A lean sell does
  too, and if it does not land, `sellWithRetry`'s one retry is sent on the
  **fast** lane: saving a fee never leaves a bag stuck. The dust guard compares
  against the fee actually paid. `manual_sell` recorder rows carry the lane and
  the priority fee, so landing can be measured per lane.
- **Scripts**: `bot.buy(mint, sol, { lane: 'lean' })` and
  `bot.sell(mint, { pct, lane: 'lean' })`. Trading wallet only (another
  wallet's trade refuses the option, like slippage). An unknown lane is
  refused. Documented in the script API table.
- **Unchanged on purpose**: every button, every advanced order (stops,
  take-profits, trailing), auto-sells, copy trading and rug exits stay on the
  fast lane.
- **Farm scripts v2.8** (bundled + both private copies): the buy and the timed
  sell use the lean lane; a sell after the creator sold stays fast. An app
  built before this change ignores the option and trades as before.

- **Paper charges the lane's fixed fees on unattended trades.** Paper used to
  book 1.5 % a side and nothing fixed, so a paper record of a 0.02 SOL script
  showed none of the ~17 % the floors cost it live. A script's or the AI
  connection's paper buy now adds `paperFixedFeeSol(lane, 'buy')` to its cost
  and a paper sell takes `paperFixedFeeSol(lane, 'sell')` from its proceeds
  (fast: floor + measured mean tip + base = 0.001061 / 0.002443 SOL; lean:
  0.000017 a side). A hand-placed paper trade is unchanged.

## Tests

`test/exitbudget.test.mjs` pins the units (micro-lamports × CU ÷ 1e15 — a
÷ 1e12 slip was caught while writing it), the clamp, the default, the guard and
that `leanExec` does not mutate the settings. `test/automation.test.mjs` pins
that the lane reaches the host on a buy and a sell, that a farm-sized 0.016 SOL
bag clears the lean guard and not the fast one, and that an unknown lane never
trades.

## Not measured / follow-ups

- **How fast a lean trade lands.** The sample shows cheap trades land, not how
  long they wait. The farm's exit is on a timer, so seconds matter little; the
  graduation scalper loses 0.4–1 pt per second and stays on the fast lane.
  Every lean sell is recorded with its timing, so the first live session
  answers it.
- On PumpSwap the builder raises the limit to 250k CU and divides the same fee
  over it, so a lean pool trade bids about half the CU price a curve trade
  does.
- `feeEstimator.fromRpc` drops zero-fee slots before taking percentiles, so its
  median is biased upward; the lean clamp makes that harmless, and the fast
  lane is floored anyway.
