# Looks: themes that move more than a colour, and My Layout → Widgets (2026-09-20)

Krypt's ask: rename My Layout to Widgets; then custom themes that are more
than colours — entirely different looks (futuristic, clean simple minimalist,
hacker) with custom fonts.

## Widgets

"My Layout" is "Widgets" everywhere a person reads it: the Hub tile, the
sidebar (`nav.widgets` in eight languages), the page title, the Links
panel's copy, the privacy policy's sentence about the Links panel, and the
tests and drivers that name it. The workspace id (`layout`) and the route
(`workspace`) did not change, so saved state and pins keep working.

## Looks

A second axis beside the accent. Where an accent moves one colour, a LOOK
moves the fonts, the surfaces, the corner radius and the effects — the
character of the screen. They combine freely. Neither moves emerald, rose,
crimson or gold: on a trading screen those are data.

- **Classic** — what the app always was: Cinzel headings, Spline Sans,
  JetBrains Mono, engraved plates with corner runes, the soft glow, stars.
- **Futuristic** — Orbitron headings (uppercase, wide-tracked), Rajdhani
  text, Share Tech Mono numbers; near-square corners; a faint grid instead
  of stars; panels edged in the accent. Slightly denser. Brings the blue
  accent along the first time.
- **Minimal** — Inter for everything, JetBrains Mono numbers; flat panels,
  no glow, no runes, no backdrops, no gradient wordmark; softer corners and
  a little more room around everything.
- **Hacker** — VT323 headings over Share Tech Mono; a pure black screen,
  square corners, scanlines over the whole shell, a blinking cursor after
  the page title, headings with a faint glow. Brings the green accent along
  the first time.

- **Retro** — an arcade cabinet: the page title in Press Start 2P with a
  hard accent shadow; headings, labels, text and numbers on Share Tech Mono,
  uppercase and tracked (Pixelify Sans was tried for text and headings and
  dropped: at table size a pixel B is an 8 and a C is an O, and "BNB" cannot
  read "8N8" on a trading screen); square corners; chunky panels with an offset drop shadow; faint
  CRT lines and a vignette closing in from the edges.
- **XP / Y2K** — the first LIGHT look. Luna blue on white with a Bliss sky
  (sky to grass) behind the shell, the system's Tahoma and Trebuchet MS,
  Lucida Console numbers, a blue stripe along the top of every panel.
  Brings the blue accent along the first time. Two things made a light look
  possible without touching a class: Tailwind's `black` now reads
  `--krypt-ink` (the ~180 `bg-black/N` input and inset washes become a
  blue-grey wash), and the colours that carry meaning are darkened for
  contrast on white through attribute-prefix overrides — green stays the up
  colour and red the down colour; only their shade moves. The chart re-inks
  its axes from the look's variables on a change.

### How it is built

- `shared/theme.ts`: `SKINS`, `SkinId`, `DEFAULT_SKIN = 'classic'`,
  `isSkinId`, `SKIN_META`. `AppSettings.skin`; the validator's enum
  refuses an unknown look; the store falls back to classic for a save from
  before looks existed.
- `src/state/skin.ts` (+ `useSkin`): the attribute `data-skin` on
  `<html>`, a localStorage mirror seeded by `initSkin()` in main.tsx before
  React mounts, subscribers. `LiteModeHost` mirrors the setting, like the
  accent.
- `tailwind.config.js`: the surfaces (`krypt-black/void/surface/panel/muted`)
  and `white` — which is the TEXT colour, parchment on Classic — now read
  variables with `<alpha-value>`, exactly as the accent does; the three font
  families read `--font-sans/display/mono`; `rounded-*` reads
  `--radius-*`. No class in the app changed; 1,036 alpha uses of the text
  colour keep working.
- `src/index.css`: Classic's values under `:root, html[data-skin='classic']`;
  every literal surface colour in the stylesheet became a variable; one
  block per other look sets the variables and restyles the few classes that
  carry Classic's character (`.plate`, `.card`, the backdrops, the glows).
  `font-size` on `<html>` per look moves the rem-based spacing, which is
  what makes Futuristic denser and Minimal airier without touching text
  sizes. Lite mode's rules carry `!important` and still win.
- Fonts: five more `@fontsource` packages, bundled like the first three —
  no remote font request, ever, so the privacy policy did not change.
- `ThemePicker`: Look above Accent. Each look's row is a real little panel
  in the look's own fonts, surfaces and corners, with the current accent as
  the one coloured thing. Picking Hacker or Futuristic brings its paired
  accent along only while the accent is still the default purple, so a
  colour someone chose is never overwritten.

### Pins

`test/theme.test.mjs`: every look defines every variable (channels for
the colours), no look block names the accent or a colour that carries
meaning, the picker previews equal the CSS, Tailwind reads the surfaces,
the fonts and the corners through variables, no literal surface colour is
left in the stylesheet, main refuses an unknown look and the store falls
back, the fonts are bundled and seeded before React. `test/skin.test.mjs`:
the store's mirror, attribute, coercion and subscribers. Live:
`npm run test:skins:e2e` screenshots the Hub, Discover and the picker in each
look and reads the fonts actually in use off the computed styles.

### Known limits

JS painters that hold a text colour literal (the holder map's hover stroke,
the games canvas) keep parchment in every look. The two WebGL scenes pick up
a look on their next mount, as they do the accent.
