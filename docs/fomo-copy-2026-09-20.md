# FOMO copying (2026-09-20)

## What it is

A third **direction** for a copy config, after `copy` and `reverse`
(`reverse-copy-2026-09-20.md`). A FOMO config follows a **set** of
wallets rather than one address, and enters when **K distinct wallets from
that set buy the same coin inside a window**:

- the set (`fomoSource`): the wallets your enabled copy/reverse configs
  follow, the wallets saved on the Wallet Scout, your tracked-wallet list,
  or the Scout's top N by Copy score (`fomoTopN`, re-read every 30 s so
  the list moves as the score does);
- the rule: `fomoMinWallets` (default 3) of them within `fomoWindowSec`
  (default 180 s); once per coin while a position is open, and once per
  window otherwise;
- the entry: a synthetic buy through the ordinary `evaluateBuy` — same
  filters, sizing (proportional sizing uses the crowd's average buy),
  delay, staleness rule, paper honesty — with the row's `triggeredBy`
  naming the wallets that fired it;
- the exits: **the crowd leaving** (`fomoCrowdExitPct`, default 50 % of the
  trigger wallets having sold the coin) plus the config's own take-profit
  / stop-loss / max hold.

Solana only: the crowd is heard on the pump curve firehose (every trade,
gated by one Set lookup) and on the followed wallets' own subscriptions.
A trade that arrives on both paths counts as one wallet.

## What the research says, and why the form says it

`wallet-convergence-2026-09-14.md`, 9.3 M trades: with 2–4 strong wallets
converging on a coin within minutes, the follower's median outcome got
**worse** with each extra wallet, −15 % → −32 % at 60 min. Convergence
is a crowd arriving, and a follower is by definition behind it. This is
the feature competitors call "FOMO copy". It ships because people ask
for it, it starts on paper, switched off, and the editor carries the
finding in an amber box. Nothing here claims it works.

## Renames that came with it

Reverse's exits became **every direction's** exits, because a FOMO
position needs them and a copy may want them:

- `reverseTakeProfitPct` / `reverseStopLossPct` / `reverseMaxHoldMin` →
  `exitTakeProfitPct` / `exitStopLossPct` / `exitMaxHoldMin`;
  `DEFAULT_REVERSE_*` → `DEFAULT_EXIT_*`; `reverseExitOf` → `ownExitsOf`
  (reverse and FOMO fill the defaults in; a copy's are **null = off** —
  their sells alone decide, exactly as before); `checkReverseExits` →
  `checkOwnExits`. No shipped config carried the old names (the feature
  was hours old), so there is no migration.
- The exit bounds now bind whatever the direction (a copy with a 99 %
  stop-loss is refused too).

## Where it lives

- `shared/copytrade.ts`: `CopyDirection` gains `'fomo'`; `FomoSource`,
  `FOMO_SOURCES`, `FOMO_SOURCE_LABEL`, the `DEFAULT_FOMO_*` constants,
  `FOMO_WALLET` (the sentinel a FOMO config carries as its `wallet`),
  `isFomo`, `fomoRuleOf`, `ownExitsOf`; the five `fomo*` config fields;
  `CopyTrade.triggeredBy`; `CopySnapshot.crowd` (per config: source and
  how many wallets it holds right now — 0 is an honest zero);
  validation (Solana only, wallet must be the sentinel, K 2–50, window
  10–3600 s, top N 2–200, crowd exit 1–100 %); `describeConfig` wording.
- `electron/engine/copyTrade.ts`: `noteCrowdTrade` (buys accumulate per
  config+mint inside the window, sells by trigger wallets count toward the
  crowd exit), `crowdWants` (the union of every enabled FOMO config's set,
  cached 30 s — the firehose asks this for every trade),
  `forgetCrowdSources` (on every upsert), `CopyHost.scoutSaved /
  scoutTop / trackedWallets`, `activeWallets` and the leader board
  exclude FOMO configs, `checkOwnExits`. Membership is compared
  lower-cased because the Scout keys its saved list that way; the record
  keeps the chain's spelling.
- `electron/engine/engine.ts`: the three host methods (Scout saved list,
  tracked list, `rankScout(..., 'copyScore')` over week summaries), and
  the firehose hook at the top of `onTrade`.
- `electron/ipc.ts` `copy:save`: the five fields; a FOMO config skips the
  address check and is forced onto the sentinel.
- `src/pages/Wallets.tsx`: a "New FOMO copy" button, the Direction
  control's third option (switching to FOMO pins the chain to Solana and
  swaps the wallet for the sentinel; switching away clears it), the
  crowd fields, the research box, own-exit fields for every direction
  (copy hints "blank = off"), a purple `fomo` badge, the card names the
  source instead of an address, the status line says how many wallets
  it is listening to (or that the set is empty), arm confirmation names
  the rule and the finding.
- Guides: one line.

## Verified

`test/copytrade.test.mjs` 136/136 (+9): three of the saved wallets fire
one entry named after them and a fourth buy does not fire a second; the
same wallet three times is one wallet and a buy that fell out of the
window does not count; the tracked source ignores saved wallets and the
top-N source takes the first n; the followed source is the enabled copy
configs, a trade heard on both paths is one wallet, and the crowd is not a
leader; 2 of 3 selling closes it with "the crowd left" on the slice, a
non-trigger wallet's sell does not count, PnL net of both sides; the
default take-profit fires at +26 % not +24 %, a copy with its own
stop-loss exits without the leader and a copy without one never does; a
paused config hears nothing and an unanswerable source is an empty crowd;
live entries buy once through the host and the crowd exit sells 100 %;
validation, defaults, the snapshot's crowd size, the description.
`test/ipccontract.test.mjs` 21/21 (29 copy fields pinned).
`test/fomo.e2e.mjs` (`npm run test:fomo:e2e`) against the dev app: the
button opens the editor, three directions, the crowd fields, the research
warning, the exits, no address field, chain pinned, four sources, the
defaults 3/180/50 and 25/20/30, Top N only for the top source; cancelled,
nothing saved. Both typechecks clean.

## Not built

- EVM crowds. The EVM leaders arrive through each chain's scanner poll;
  the crowd machinery is chain-agnostic but nothing feeds it there.
- A FOMO figure on the Copy score, or any measurement of the crowd exit.
  The convergence study is the only number, and it is negative.
- "Only new coins" / minimum crowd size in SOL. The ordinary copy filters
  (min liquidity, pump-only, per-minute cap, daily limits) apply as they
  do to a copy.
